ARCHITECTURE & EXPERIENCE

Engineered for daily life, built to run bare-metal.

01

Built for Daily-Life Word Lookups

Quickly looking up everyday phrases and seeing what each word actually means is why Gloss exists. WordSegmenter splits source and translated sentences into tappable words powered by bidirectional lookup and an in-memory LRU cache.

WordSegmenter · LRU Cache
02

Offline for Accessibility & Reach

Running NLLB-200-distilled-600M INT8 ONNX on-device via onnxruntime-android 1.32.0 keeps 202 languages—including underserved ones—working anywhere without Wi-Fi, roaming data, accounts, or paywalls on Android 7.0+ phones, with privacy as a built-in bonus.

FLORES-200 · SHA-256 Verified
03

Home Screen Widgets

Translate before opening a full screen with the 4×1 Quick Translate bar (instant paste and language swap) and the 4×2 Recent Phrases widget routed cleanly through singleTop intents.

AppWidgetProvider · 4×1 & 4×2
04

Signed In-Place Updates

Compiled in CI with a persistent release keystore so every update installs cleanly over older APKs without losing your downloaded 658 MB model, while an in-app GitHub Releases checker handles 1-tap APK installation.

Persistent Keystore · FileProvider
